Stories from the lane

Listen to the sounds that start every trail day, then read how the stalls came to be.

Market opening jingle

Why we built the trail

Our group kept eating at the same two places near campus. We walked the whole lane to find out what else was hiding, and wrote down everything that was worth the trip.

How we picked stops

Each stall had to be family-run, cheap enough for a student budget, and open at a different time of day so the route works from morning to night.
